> Sample procedure for the Smart Ward demo. Fictional, not clinical advice,
> and not a substitute for your organisation's own policies.

## When this applies

The monitor in a medication storage area reads relative humidity below 30 %
or above 60 % for two readings in a row. The system records humidity with
every reading but does not open a Case for it on its own: a nurse or the
agent notices it from the device's readings, and a person opens the Case.

## Steps

1. Ask the agent for the storage area's latest readings and confirm at least
   two readings outside 30 to 60 %.
2. Check the obvious causes: a door propped open, a humidifier or kettle
   nearby, a leak, a cleaning cart with wet mops parked against the cabinet.
3. Close the cabinet and move anything that adds moisture. Recheck after
   30 minutes.
4. If humidity is still out of range, open a Case for the pharmacy lead
   (or ask the agent to escalate the device's condition) and say how long it
   has been out of range.
5. Pharmacy decides whether any stock is moved or quarantined. Ward staff do
   not discard or relabel stock themselves.

## Escalate when

- Humidity has been above 70 % for more than one hour.
- Packaging is visibly damp, swollen or stuck together.
- The same storage area was out of range twice in one week.
